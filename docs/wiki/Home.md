# StageSignal Wiki

StageSignal is a planned Windows live-performance visualiser, video mixer, show controller, recorder and lighting controller.
The specification (`StageSignal-Specification-v0.1.md`, version 0.1 revision 1) is the design baseline. Its numbers are targets, not
measurements.
